[[NARRATIVE]]
They do not wake naturally.

There is no gradual return — no slow assembly of awareness, no comfortable progression from sleep to surface. One moment there is nothing, and the next moment there is everything, all at once, too loud and too close and too immediate for the transition to be called waking up in any normal sense. It is more like being placed back into consciousness by something that did not particularly care about the gentleness of the landing.

The animals are already there.
